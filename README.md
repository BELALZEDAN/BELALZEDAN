## 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

I am a **Computer & Automatic Control Engineer** operating at the intersection of **Cyber-Physical Systems (Industrial Automation/SCADA)** and **Decentralized Cryptographic Architectures (Web3 & DeFi)**. 

My core focus spans rigorous **Smart Contract Invariant Auditing** using Foundry, architecting resilient EVM protocols, building high-throughput scalable Full-Stack platforms, and engineering deterministic control systems with Siemens Simatic PLCs.

- 🌐 **Official Engineering Portfolio**: [https://belal-zedan-portfolio.vercel.app/](https://belal-zedan-portfolio.vercel.app/)
- 🛡️ **DeFi Security & Auditing**: Specialized in invariant testing, EVM gas optimization, reentrancy defense, and multi-chain oracles (Chainlink CCIP/VRF).
- ⚙️ **Industrial Automation**: Deterministic automation logic, Siemens Simatic S7-300/400 PLC programming, SCADA twins, and sensor-actuator integration.
- 🚀 **Full-Stack Engineering**: Production-ready microservices, reactive architectures (React, TailwindCSS), robust backends (Node.js, Laravel), and RESTful APIs.
- 📍 **Location**: Damascus, Syria 🇸🇾 | Open to remote worldwide engineering & auditing engagements.
